Down below are a list of every cog that you can
spawn in this game, to spawn a cog, simply use
the command ~spawncog (prefix) (level) (revives)
in the chat box. Prefix is the letter in front
of the cog's name shown below, you can spawn
the cogs from the level pools also listed below, 
you can't spawn a cog outside of the cog's level
pool, if you try to spawn one above the cog's
level pool it will only go to it's max level,
revives is to spawn a cog with multiple revives 
or version 2 cogs, but you can spawn them with as
many layers as you like. Version 2 cogs are a 
little buggy atm so I wouldn't suggest spawning 
them until they are completely fixed.
